# Tutor phrases: john_lansink2024, 2026-09-11

## Interview questions

- **mentor / onboard a new developer** — "*Have you ever had to* mentor a junior engineer or *onboard a new developer to your team*? And *how do you normally approach it*?"
- **tell me about a time you had to** — "*Tell me about a time you had to* learn a new framework, library, or tool quickly *for a project or some initiative*."
- **during sprint planning** — "*How do you estimate how long* a front end task or user story *would take during sprint planning*?"

## Prioritization and team process

- **take precedence over** — "Usually, by definition, client work *takes precedence over* everything."
- **competing activities** — "We have our own responsibility to verify the priority of *competing activities* using priority methodologies like RICE, for instance."
- **have established processes in place** — "By using such methodologies and *having established processes in place*, we can extract business values and impact from the current situation…"
- **job shadow** — "You assign a junior engineer to *job shadow* with one of your senior engineers."

## Estimation

- **stick your finger up in the air** — "You're not just going to *stick your finger up in the air* and say, this EPIC is going to take three weeks." (This means making a wild guess. It does not mean raising your hand.)
- **compile and tally** — "Usually once you *compile and tally* all those numbers, that gives you the main estimate of the overall goal."
- **know it inside and out** — "If the team *knows it inside and out*, then it's going to be quick."

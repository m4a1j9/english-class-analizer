## Running a session

- **a dry run** — "I think it was time to just do a little bit of *a dry run*, warm up on some of the questions."
- **a debrief** — "We'll do one more, and then we'll do a little bit of *a debrief*."
- **at your discretion** — "We won't go through the terms, but you can *at your discretion*."

## Interview questions

- **Why don't we start off with… / tell us a little bit about yourself** — "*Why don't we start off with* — if you could *tell us a little bit about yourself*, your background, your experience as a front-end engineer — *I'd love to hear*."
- **making a career move / what's the driver** — "*Why are you interested in making a career move right now?*" … "So *what's the driver for you* wanting to make a move right now into another position?"
- **ensure smooth communication and alignment** — "So *how would you ensure smooth communication and alignment when working with* back-end developers?"
- **stands out in your experience** — "Is there one that *stands out in your experience* that *you'd like to highlight*?"
- **how do you normally approach it** — "*Do you have any actual experience* mentoring junior engineers or onboarding a new developer to your team? *If so, how do you normally approach it?*"
- **justify / come to these values** — "*How do you justify* these percentages? *How did you actually come to these values?*"
- **explain to a non-technical person** — "*How do you explain the difference to a non-technical person?*"

## Describing work and business impact

- **near the finish line** — "You're *near the finish line*."
- **winding down** — "And now is actually the perfect time, now that they're *winding down*, to look for something new."
- **a stepping stone** — "But I think now is the perfect *stepping stone* for something new."
- **collaborate cross-functionally** — "You mentioned you're part of a few different development teams, and you *collaborate cross-functionally*."
- **client-facing meetings** — "More and more *resources* are doing multiple things, including being brought to *client-facing meetings*, especially if you're really good at what you do." (*resources* here means people/staff, not tools or materials)
- **save a significant amount of time** — "We built a workflow that fully automated a manual workflow or process that *saved a significant amount of time*."
- **very well received** — "You talked about the major features that you worked on that were *very well received*."
- **from an architectural perspective** — "You certainly would … talk about a particular feature or initiative *from an architectural perspective*, both from front and back end."
- **address something in a reasonable amount of time** — "Or is this something that we can *address in a reasonable amount of time*?"
- **put that kind of hat on** — "So they want to see you being able to *put that kind of hat on* when you're answering this question."

## Feedback
